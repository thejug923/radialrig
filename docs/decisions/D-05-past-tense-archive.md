**Status: in-force.** Grade: decided. The support is the owner's
judgment that compliance costs near zero, which is argument, not
observation; SPEC-1 section 3 gives an `assumed` record a `verify-by`
event, and this record never had one.

## Ruling

Archived records use the past tense and open with a line stating they are a
record of a past position, not an instruction.

## Why

An agent does not cleanly separate "document I retrieved" from "instruction
I was given." Imperative text in a dead record still commands. Rewriting it
as history removes the hazard at the source. See the testbed research
corpus.
