**Status: in-force.**

## Ruling

Generated summaries, indexes, and views are disposable and regenerable.
They never hold authority, and no governed record may cite one as its
source. Authority lives only in authored, reviewed records.

## Why

A summary that acquires authority becomes an untraceable ruling. Summaries
lose conditions, scope, and negation without showing the loss. See the
testbed research corpus on compression and reflection loss.
