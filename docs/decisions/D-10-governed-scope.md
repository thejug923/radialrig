**Status: in-force.**

## Ruling

Decision records exist to prevent detrimental regression: the risk of
losing ratified or existing behavior without a clear redirection overruling
the decision. A question is admitted to governance only when losing or
contradicting its answer would carry that risk.

A candidate record governing fringe cases or test behavior receives double
scrutiny before admission.

## Why

Too coarse, and records become essays nobody rewrites. Too fine, and the
governed set fills with trivia that dilutes the records that matter. The
regression test keeps admission tied to real cost. Ruled by the owner on
2026-08-14; no reviewed research settles granularity, so this is a project
ruling.
