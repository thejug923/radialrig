**Status: in-force.**

## Ruling

The minimal spec and the pilot fixture come before the full spec and the
validator. The spec grows only when a pilot result or a real defect demands
it. This record is the former SD-2.

## Why

The design rests on assumptions about agent behavior (see D-03, D-04).
Building the full apparatus before measuring them risks hardening a wrong
design. The pilot is the cheapest instrument that can falsify the
assumptions.
